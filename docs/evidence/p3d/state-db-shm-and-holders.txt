The tick is every five minutes, on :x2:22, and ~/.local/state/<desktop-shell>/agents/usage/hermes.json moves in the same second
(22:57:22.65 against the 22:57:23.08 shm touch; 23:12:21.80 against 23:12:21.59). The desktop shell's agents collector (/usr/share/<desktop-shell>/shell/plugins/agents/collectors.py) opens
state.db read-only (sqlite3.connect(... '?mode=ro')), which touches the -shm index file without changing state.db.
So the shm movement predates the spike and continued after it; it does not correlate with the spike.

fuser on the real state.db and state.db-shm was empty at every probe (the snapshots and probes summarized in its README.md), and a
39-sample scan of every <user> process's fds at 23:12:05-23:12:45 (poll-after-teardown output below), straddling the
23:12:21 tick, caught no holder. The collector's open is shorter than the ~1 s a full /proc scan takes, so the holder
at the tick was NOT captured. The expected holder (hermes_bridge.py, pid 1270) was running throughout but held no fd on
state.db at any sample. State plainly: I attribute the shm movement to the collector from the cadence and the code, not
from catching its fd.
